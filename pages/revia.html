Before you start

Use non-sensitive demo input. Your text travels over HTTPS through a relay to the developer’s computer; the relay can read it. This is not end-to-end encrypted. The operator and hosting infrastructure may have visibility into traffic.

Guest conversations use temporary, isolated context and are not stored as durable owner memory. Application-owned guest data is cleared on end or expiry; hosting/CDN access logs have their own retention policies. This page keeps the session token in memory only. Ending the session clears this transcript; browser or device software may retain its own copies.

The website cannot control your computer or the developer’s computer. Guests have no access to desktop actions, private memory, screen or camera content, files, or other conversations. Replies are generated AI output and can be inaccurate.

When live chat is offline, explore the source and architecture above. No recorded exchange is presented as a live reply.
